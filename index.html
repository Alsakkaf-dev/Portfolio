About Me

My journey and philosophy

The Journey Begins

I'm Mohammed Alsakkaf, a software engineering student from Yemen, now studying in Malaysia. I'm honest about where I am: still in the early stages of learning (I know some C++ and Java basics). But what I lack in technical experience, I make up for with determination, creativity, and a clear vision of what I want to build.

My strongest skill isn't coding yet—it's problem-solving and creative thinking. I can visualize solutions, understand what needs to be built, and use the right tools (including AI) to bring those ideas to life. I'm learning to be a director of technology, not just a coder.
